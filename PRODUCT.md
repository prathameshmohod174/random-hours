## Platform

web

## Users

Potential collaborators, creative studios, and clients looking for an independent filmmaker or visual storyteller.

## Product Purpose

Random Hours is Prathamesh Mohod's personal portfolio. It presents his film, cinematography, photography, editing, and personal visual work so visitors can understand his practice, explore projects, and initiate a conversation or collaboration.

## Positioning

The portfolio distinguishes projects by the actual contribution (including co-direction, cinematography, photography, and editing-only work) and lets the visual work lead rather than presenting a conventional resume.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive to browse selected work, understand Prathamesh's creative approach and capabilities, then contact him by email or Instagram. Projects link to their original video or social post where available.

## Brand Commitments

The identity is Random Hours, with the tagline "Stories told in frames." The requested personality is cinematic, atmospheric, minimal but expressive, thoughtful, personal, and confident without feeling corporate. The requested visual direction is dark, high-contrast, editorial, and film-inspired.

## Product Principles

- Keep role attribution precise and factual.
